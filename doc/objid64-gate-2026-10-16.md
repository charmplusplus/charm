## Result in one paragraph

Every objid-path test passes on both machines after one fix: `tests/charm++/objid_insert`
hung (production) or asserted (debug) on Anvil in the "re-creation of destroyed
elements" step because `CkLocMgr::duringDestruction` was never initialized, so
`reclaim()` skipped the `reclaimRemote` message to the home whenever the heap byte
was non-zero. The bug predates the redesign (it is on classic and recharm); the
reconverse bump to cf7f5a9 (#269) changed the startup heap layout on Linux and
exposed it. Fix: PR #4044 (two lines, verified 20/20 on Anvil including the shapes
that hung). The Mac never reproduced it. Everything else that fails is a stale test
or example that no longer compiles against reconverse, a known reconverse/LCI
limitation, or a test-setup mismatch; see the classification below.
